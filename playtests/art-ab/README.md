# Art A/B playtest (built files only)

One small walkable clearing with the same mech, movement, camera and collision in two candidate art
directions: **1 Gouache (Amber Quiet)** and **4 Woodcut (The Printed Forest)**. Press 1 / 4 or tap the
labels to switch without resetting. WebGPU is required. This is a comparison build, not the game's adopted look.
